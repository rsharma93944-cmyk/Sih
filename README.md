# 🌍 ResQAI

## Nature Warns. We Act.

ResQAI is an AI-powered disaster management platform designed to support **Early Warning Systems, Landslide Risk Monitoring, Disaster Response, Rescue Coordination, and Disaster Intelligence**.

The project primarily focuses on **North East India (NER)**, a region that is highly vulnerable to landslides and other natural disasters due to heavy rainfall, mountainous terrain, steep slopes, and changing environmental conditions.

ResQAI brings together environmental monitoring, artificial intelligence, risk analysis, early warnings, disaster response, and rescue coordination into one unified platform.

---

# 🚀 Project Overview

ResQAI combines two major objectives:

### 1. AI Disaster Response & Rescue Coordinator

A centralized platform for monitoring disaster incidents, coordinating emergency response teams, managing rescue operations, and tracking emergency resources.

### 2. AI-Based Early Warning and Landslide Risk Monitoring System in North East India

A system designed to analyze environmental and geographical conditions to identify potential landslide risks and generate early warnings.
